Process

Twenty-one working days, in writing

Every quote comes with a day-by-day schedule. Here is how a typical ensuite runs.

  1. Week 0

    Site visit & fixed quote

    We measure, check the plumbing, and send a fixed price within five days.

  2. Days 1–3

    Demolition & rough-in

    Strip to studs, move drains and supply lines, add circuits and blocking.

  3. Days 4–9

    Waterproofing & tile

    Membrane, flood test, then slab walls, floor tile and the shower niche.

  4. Days 10–17

    Millwork & lighting

    Panels, vanity, mirror and LED, installed and wired by our own trades.

  5. Days 18–21

    Fixtures & handover

    Toilet, glass and trim, a full clean, and a walkthrough with your warranty.
